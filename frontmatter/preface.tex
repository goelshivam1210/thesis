\chapter*{Preface}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Preface}

All work presented in this dissertation was conducted in the Multi-Modal Interaction and Perception Laboratory (MuLIP Lab) in the Department of Computer Science at Tufts University, in close association with the Human-Robot Interaction Laboratory (HRI Lab), under the supervision of Professor Jivko Sinapov and Professor Matthias Scheutz.

Portions of this dissertation are based on work that has been previously published or submitted for publication. The following list identifies those chapters and summarizes the author's contributions relative to the associated collaborative publications.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \Cref{ch:foundations} contains material adapted from \citep{goel2024neurosymbolic}. I co-led the paper with Panagiotis Lymperopoulos and contributed to the conceptual framing, formalization, system design, implementation, evaluation, and writing. The remaining co-authors contributed to the broader architecture, experimental infrastructure, evaluation, advising, and writing.

    \item \Cref{ch:benchmarks} contains material originally published in \citep{goel2021novelgridworlds,goel2024novelgym}. For \citep{goel2021novelgridworlds}, I co-led the design of the benchmark with Gyan Tatiya; Jivko Sinapov and Matthias Scheutz contributed advising and writing. For \citep{goel2024novelgym}, I led the benchmark design, evaluation, experimentation, and writing. Yichen Wei and Klara Chura contributed to the implementation, and Panagiotis Lymperopoulos, Jivko Sinapov, and Matthias Scheutz contributed advising and writing.

    \item \Cref{ch:rapid_learn} contains material originally published in \citep{goel2022rapidlearn} and material adapted from \citep{goel2024neurosymbolic}. For \citep{goel2022rapidlearn}, I co-led the paper with Yash Shukla and contributed to the algorithmic design, implementation, experimental evaluation, and writing. Vasanth Sarathy, Matthias Scheutz, and Jivko Sinapov contributed advising and writing. For the material adapted from \citep{goel2024neurosymbolic}, I contributed to the integration of RAPid-Learn within the larger neurosymbolic architecture.

    \item \Cref{ch:flex} contains material originally published in \citep{fang2025flex}. I contributed to the conceptual framing, experimental analysis, and writing, and adapted the work for its role in this dissertation. Shijie Fang and Wenchang Gao co-led the algorithmic design, implementation, and experiments; Christopher Thierauf contributed implementation and experimental support; Matthias Scheutz and Jivko Sinapov contributed advising and writing.

    \item \Cref{ch:pho} contains material from \citep{goel2025pho}, which was submitted for publication. I led the algorithmic design, implementation, experimental evaluation, and writing. Timothy Duggan co-led the implementation, design and writing with me, and Tomas Maranga contributed experimental support; Matthias Scheutz and Jivko Sinapov contributed advising and writing.
\end{itemize}

The remaining chapters synthesize, motivate, and contextualize these contributions within the broader dissertation argument. All adaptations were made to produce a unified presentation of the dissertation's central thesis: that structured abstractions enable autonomous agents and robots to adapt and generalize in open-world environments.
